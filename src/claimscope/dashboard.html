Portfolio mix / Direct standardization

How much does the age mix change the comparison?

Apply the same portfolio-wide driver-age distribution to every supported region. Compare crude frequency with an age-standardized rate before interpreting a regional ranking.

CrudeAge-standardized

Claims per 100 policy-years. A common age distribution removes measured age composition only. Region differences can still reflect other products, drivers, vehicles, or selection.

95% standardized intervals use independent Poisson cell counts and a normal approximation. Regions failing any age-band exposure requirement remain visible as unsupported; their rates are not imputed.

A standardized rate is a descriptive comparison, not a causal regional effect or an underwriting recommendation. Rankings are exploratory and do not adjust for multiple comparisons.

04 / Held-out diagnostic

Measure model performance—and its dependencies

Regularized Poisson regression predicts annual claim frequency. Exposure weights make a short policy contribute proportionally less than a full policy-year.

Calibration across held-out risk deciles

ObservedPredicted

Claims per 100 policy-years; deciles use predicted rate. Tied predictions are deterministically ordered by policy ID. The downloadable CSV preserves exposure and counts.

Evaluation design

Features: driver age band, vehicle age band, bonus/malus band, area, fuel, region, and vehicle power. Identifiers and amount-record outcomes are excluded.

Policy ID hashing separates train / validation / test. No dates or customer linkage are available, so this is neither temporal validation nor evidence of generalization to new customers. Bonus/malus availability at underwriting is unverified.

Does the result depend on bonus/malus information?

A frozen sensitivity model removes bonus/malus, whose availability at original underwriting is unverified. The original split and selected regularization are unchanged. This extension follows inspection of the original holdout; it is not fresh validation.

Exploratory benchmark only. No premium quoting, underwriting, protected-group fairness conclusion, or production model endpoint. Lower deviance measures average count fit, not profit.
05 / Reproducibility

Definitions that travel with the dashboard

Metric dictionary

  • Policy-years: sum of source exposure, without clipping values above one.
  • Frequency: reported claim count ÷ policy-years.
  • Recorded cost / year: linked recorded amounts ÷ policy-years.
  • Mean recorded claim: linked recorded amounts ÷ linked amount-record count.
  • Loss ratio: unavailable, because earned premium is absent.

Amount units are left as source monetary units; inflation and valuation dates are not available. Some amounts follow French IRSA-IDA settlement conventions.

Real source, pinned version

CASdatasets · freMTPL2freq and freMTPL2sev. Anonymized French motor third-party liability records from a private insurer.
